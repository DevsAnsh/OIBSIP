FORMULAS

The math behind the measure.

Every result uses standard scientific conversions, rounded only for display.

Celsius → Fahrenheit °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
Fahrenheit → Celsius °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
Celsius → Kelvin K = °C + 273.15
Fahrenheit → Kelvin K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15
A LITTLE SCIENCE

Nothing gets colder than this.

Absolute zero is the lowest theoretical temperature: 0 K, equal to −273.15 °C or −459.67 °F.
